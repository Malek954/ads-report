September & October Plan

Why this plan - 4 facts from the data

  • The Best-Seller catalog is the #1 ad by far - EGP 210,775 and 115 orders in the last 30 days (18.8x, or 14.6x on normal days without the Sep 12 sale). Its text still says "summer styles" - it needs autumn wording.
  • Full-price single-piece videos beat the sale videos. On normal days the Boho Kimono video returned 18.5x and the Linen Shirt video 18.5x (EGP 2,688 per order), while the two live "Sale Collection" videos returned 10.1x and 4.8x. But the Kimono ad has a delivery issue and Linen Shirt is paused.
  • September's discount is already used. The one-day "50% off everything" on Sep 12 brought about a quarter of the month's revenue, but at about EGP 1,400 per order vs EGP 1,778 on the full-price catalog. No more sales this month.
  • The account was off last September-October, so this plan follows the Egyptian calendar: back-to-school season now, Armed Forces Day on Oct 6, then Black Friday prep. Saturday is the best day (14.2x) and 11am-4pm the best buying hours.

5 rules for the owner

  1. Never discount the best-sellers. The catalog sells at full price at 14.6-18.8x - discounts go to slow styles only.
  2. No more "50% off everything". It sold, but at about EGP 1,400 per order and it teaches customers to wait. One short, small offer per month at most.
  3. COD + free shipping over EGP 2,500 in every single ad. They are the #1 trust lever and the way to lift the order value.
  4. Launch everything Saturday at 11am. Saturday is the best day and 11am-4pm is when Egyptian women buy.
  5. Judge results on week-old numbers, never the last 2-3 days (they always look low before purchases finish counting).
