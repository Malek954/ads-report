October & November Plan

Why this plan - 4 facts from the data

  • The account returned 9.78x on EGP 26,388 in the last 30 days - 154 orders at an average of EGP 1,676. That average is the one number to fix: it sits below the EGP 2,500 free-shipping line, so every ad must say the line out loud.
  • Full price beats the sale, clearly. The Set video returned 31.64x, Gabardine Wide-Leg 16.77x, the Winter catalog 12.69x and the New Arrivals catalog 10.74x (EGP 94,255, the biggest earner). The live Sale catalog returned only 6.75x and the sale video 1.44x.
  • Two of the three best ads are switched off. The Set video (31.64x) and the Knitted Kimono video (9.06x) are both paused. Turning them back on is the cheapest win available this month.
  • Saturday is worth double any other day - 18.12x against 9-12x for the rest of the week. The buying window is 9am to 8pm, and midnight to 2am is the worst time to spend (4.5x). Days 8-14 are the strongest stretch of the month (15.88x), days 15-21 the weakest (8.55x).

5 rules for the owner

  1. Never discount the best-sellers or the new drop. Full price returns 10.7x to 31.6x while the sale catalog returns 6.75x - discounts go to older and slower styles only.
  2. One short discount window per month, and nothing in between. October's is already used. November's is Black Friday, Nov 26-30. No bundles, ever.
  3. COD + free shipping over EGP 2,500 in every single ad. The average order is EGP 1,676 - sitting just under the line is exactly where that message pays.
  4. Launch everything Saturday at 11am. Saturday returns 18.12x against 9-12x for every other day, and the buying window is 9am to 8pm. Pull spend off midnight to 2am (4.5x).
  5. Judge results on week-old numbers, never the last 2-3 days (they always look low before purchases finish counting).
