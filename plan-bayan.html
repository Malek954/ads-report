August & September Plan

Why this plan - 4 facts from the data

  • The last week of August is the biggest week of the year. Last year Aug 22-28 returned 4.15x and 70 orders - the best week of the whole year. The autumn collection launches exactly there.
  • Early September is the worst stretch of the year. Last year Sep 5-11 fell to 1.37x and Sep 12-18 to 1.08x. We cut budget through it instead of fighting it, then come back for late September.
  • Best-Seller and retention ads carry the account - 4.6x to 5.8x return, while cold prospecting sits near 1.3x. One Best-Seller ad runs every single week.
  • Curvy is the new winner - 6.8x on low frequency (1.9). It has barely spent yet. Scale it now while it is still fresh.
AugustTheme: scale the new winners · clear stock through the dip · launch autumn into the biggest week of the year
Week 1 Aug 1 - 7
Push winners · Cut the losers
  • Scale Curvy (6.8x) and RM (5.3x) - both new, both at low frequency, both barely funded. This is the fastest money in the account.
  • Stop the four money-losers today: FJ 5-12 (1.3x on $841), T_Len (1.2x on $822), the three MESSAGE campaign ads (0 tracked sales on $886) and Event_7-13 (0.37% CTR).
  • Week 1 returned 3.65x last year - full price, no discount, just more budget behind what already works.
Week 2-3 Aug 8 - 21
Summer clearance · 20-25%
  • This is the annual dip - last year Aug 8-14 was 2.18x and Aug 15-21 was 2.17x. Do not fight it with more budget; use it to clear stock.
  • "تصفية الصيف - آخر القطع" on last-season and slow styles only. Real scarcity - sizes running out. The winners stay full price.
  • Shoot the autumn collection now. All videos finished by Aug 18 so the launch has fresh creative ready.
Week 4 Aug 22 - 31
🚀 Autumn Collection Launch · Full price
  • Launch Saturday Aug 22. Saturday is the account's best day (3.49x) and last year this exact week hit 4.15x with 70 orders - the peak of the year. New product, never a discount.
  • VIP early access Thu-Fri Aug 20-21 for past buyers on WhatsApp, before the public launch.
  • Full launch push: new videos + Best-Seller ad + retarget everyone who browsed the clearance with the new collection.

SeptemberTheme: ride the launch · survive the early-month crash on low budget · come back for Saudi National Day
Week 1 Sep 1 - 4
Ride the launch
  • Keep the autumn launch running at full budget - last year this stretch still returned 2.96x.
  • Retarget everyone who viewed the collection during launch week but did not buy.
Week 2-3 Sep 5 - 18
⚠ Danger zone · Cut budget, hold
  • The worst two weeks of the year. Last year Sep 5-11 returned 1.37x and Sep 12-18 returned 1.08x - below break-even. Cut daily budget by roughly 30-40%.
  • Do not launch anything and do not discount. A sale here trains customers to wait and still will not move volume. Spend goes to retention and retarget only - warm buyers keep converting when cold traffic will not.
  • Use the quiet fortnight to shoot autumn/winter content and prepare the Saudi National Day push.
Week 4 Sep 19 - 30
Saudi National Day · 15% + free express
  • Demand recovers on its own - last year Sep 19-25 came back to 2.96x and Sep 26-30 to 2.68x. Put the budget back up from Sep 19.
  • Saudi National Day (Sep 23) - themed offer, 15% on selected styles plus free express delivery to KSA. Saudi is your smallest market and the one with the most room to grow.
  • Late September: tease the winter collection to build anticipation into October.

5 rules for the owner

  1. Never discount the winners. Curvy, RM, FJ and Len sell at full price - discounts go to slow styles only.
  2. Only 2 sale windows in 2 months. More sales and customers learn to wait. Bayan sells newness and quality.
  3. Aug 22 is the most important date of the year. Last year that week returned 4.15x and 70 orders. Everything in August builds toward it.
  4. In early September cut spend, not price. Sep 5-18 ran 1.08-1.37x last year. Protect the cash, do not chase it.
  5. Judge results on week-old numbers, never on the last 2-3 days (they always look low before purchases finish counting).
