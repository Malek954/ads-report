October & November Plan

Why this plan - 4 facts from the data

  • Sales campaigns returned 5.31x on $3,746 in the last 30 days - 108 orders at an average of $184. The WhatsApp campaign ($270) is left out on purpose: it is built for chat replies, so the pixel cannot value its sales.
  • Razan is the best new piece, Reva is the workhorse. Razan returned 10.85x on only $177 - the highest return in the account. Reva returned 8.23x and 5.88x, FJ 4.92x. The Noura reminder made 7.98x and is paused - turn it back on.
  • Last year, Oct 15-21 was the worst week of these two months (2.26x) and Nov 5-11 was the best (13.69x). White Friday week (Nov 26-30) returned 9.58x. We are in the weak week right now.
  • The money lands at month end. Over 90 days, days 22-31 returned 5.40x against 3.35x for days 1-7 and 3.71x for days 15-21. Spend follows the salary cycle, not the calendar week.

5 rules for the owner

  1. Never discount Razan, Reva or FJ. They are what sells right now - the clearance and White Friday are for slow and older stock only.
  2. Two discount windows in two months - Oct 17-21 and Nov 26-30. Nothing else. Bayan sells newness, and the winter collection never goes on sale.
  3. Push hardest Oct 22-31 and Nov 6-11, then again Nov 26-30. Those are the weeks the data says pay back - 5.4x, 13.7x and 9.6x.
  4. In a weak week cut spend, not price. Days 1-7 and 15-21 are the soft stretches. Trim about 20% and wait for the salary cycle.
  5. Judge results on week-old numbers, never on the last 2-3 days (they always look low before purchases finish counting).
