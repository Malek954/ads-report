IMPACT·SYSTEMS · Bayan - Monthly Marketing Plan

September & October Plan

Why this plan - 4 facts from the data

  • FJ is carrying the account. The free-size FJ abaya returned 5.84x and $6,460 in the last 30 days - the #1 ad. Sales campaigns overall returned 4.18x on $4,068 (WhatsApp campaign left out - the pixel cannot see chat sales).
  • Mid-month is usually the weak spot. Over the last 90 days, days 15-21 returned 2.88x against 4.42x for days 22-31 (salaries land at month-end). We are in that week now - trim budget about 20%, then restore it for payday.
  • Sep 22-28 was the best week of these two months last year - 7.15x. It lines up with Saudi National Day (Wednesday Sep 23), and Wednesday is the account's best day (4.28x).
  • Oct 13-19 was the worst week - 1.82x. That is half the week before (3.67x) and under a third of the week after (6.35x). The only discount of the two months goes there.
SeptemberTheme: trim through mid-month · full push for Saudi National Day · full budget into payday week
Now Sep 17 - 21
Mid-month dip · Trim 20%
  • Trim daily budgets about 20% until Sunday Sep 21. No launches and no discounts - this is the normal mid-month dip, not a problem to fix.
  • Keep FJ (5.84x) and the Best-Seller catalog (6.39x) fully funded - trim the weaker ads, never these two.
  • Finish the National Day videos by Sep 21.
Week 4 Sep 22 - 28
🚀 Saudi National Day push · Full price
  • Budget back to full on Tuesday Sep 22. Push hardest from Wednesday Sep 23, 9pm Kuwait time - the best day (4.28x) and the best hour (8.8x). Last year this week returned 7.15x.
  • Saudi National Day offer = free express delivery to KSA, at full price. No price cut - Bayan only discounts at mid-season, clearance or White Friday.
  • Lead with FJ and Noura (the curvy-cut lined abaya) - the two styles that are selling now.
Payday Sep 29 - 30
Payday week · Full budget
  • Salaries land - keep full budget and carry it straight into October. Last year Sep 29-Oct 5 returned 5.94x.

5 rules for the owner

  1. Never discount FJ or Noura. They are what sells right now - the clearance is for slow styles only.
  2. One price discount in two months - Oct 13-19. Saudi National Day gets free express delivery, not a price cut. Bayan sells newness.
  3. Push hardest Sep 22-30 and Oct 20-31. Last year those weeks returned 5.9-7.2x and 4.9-6.4x.
  4. Mid-month, cut spend - not price. Days 15-21 are usually the weakest week. Trim about 20%, then restore for payday.
  5. Judge results on week-old numbers, never on the last 2-3 days (they always look low before purchases finish counting).
